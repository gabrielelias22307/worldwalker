DRY SEASON
6:40 AM

Pick a seed in the bottom bar, then walk to a patch of soil and press Space to plant. Each band of the mountain has its own weather, so a crop that grows well in one band may dry up or freeze in another.

WHAT THE STUDENT SEES

A farm on the side of a mountain in the highlands of Colombia or Guatemala, like Stardew Valley. The mountain is cut into four bands. It gets colder the higher you climb. A clock shows the day and the season.

WHAT THEY LEARN HERE

  • The four elevation zones of Latin America: tierra caliente, templada, fría, helada
  • Why coffee grows in the mountains and not on the hot coast
  • How people match crops to the land (bananas low, potatoes high)

HOW IT PLAYS

Plant each seed and watch it grow over a few game days. A plant in the wrong band wilts and Pip explains why. A full harvest in every band fills 🏞️ What it is like and 🌱 People & land, and the last coffee sack hides the atlas page.

Ties to the reading the class already has: Why Coffee Grows in the Mountains (Quick Read 2.2).

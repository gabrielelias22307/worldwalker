Title and sign-in

WORLDWALKER

The title scene moves on its own. Try the look picker, the language buttons and the three buttons under the sign-in boxes.

WHAT THE STUDENT SEES

A short moving title scene: their hero and Pip on a hill, torn atlas pages blowing across the sky, and the Smudge hiding in a bush. Under it: pick a look, sign in, press Continue.

WHAT THEY LEARN HERE

  • The story: the Smudge tore the Great Atlas into 24 pages. Each episode wins one page back.
  • Their hero is theirs: skin, hair and hat colors carry into every episode.

HOW IT PLAYS

Sign-in is three things: class code, first name, and a 4-digit secret number. No last name, no email, no student ID. The Google Sheet only ever holds "GEO-3B · Ana · ####", so a stranger who saw it could not tell who anyone is. The class code tells you which class period the row belongs to.

Offline or Google blocked? "Play without saving online" still works. The game saves in the browser on that Chromebook, and the end screen gives a Passport Code (like WW-7K3Q-M2XA-9PDE) to paste into Canvas. Next time, they paste the code back in and keep going.

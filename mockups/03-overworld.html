WORLDWALKER MOCKUP 3 of 10

Exploring a region (Episode 1.1)

QUEST
Find 3 map pages the Smudge blew away. Use the compass rose on the sign: the first page is north of the bridge.

Arrow keys or WASD to walk (a touch pad shows up on Chromebooks with touch screens). Walk into the sign to read it. Walk into a person to talk.

WHAT THE STUDENT SEES

One screen of the map at a time, like the first Zelda. Walk off the edge and the next screen slides in. The top bar shows hearts, coins and the five geography meters.

WHAT THEY LEARN HERE

  • Cardinal directions and the compass rose
  • Reading a map key (the sign is the key)
  • Absolute vs. relative location ("north of the bridge")

HOW IT PLAYS

Each map page is hidden by a clue that uses a map skill. Picking up a page fills the 📍 Where meter. The Smudge chases you; touch it and you lose a heart, answer its question and it melts.

STANDARDS

WG.1 map skillsWG.2 regions

BUILT WITH

Tiles and characters are drawn in code (no picture files), so a whole screen costs a few hundred bytes of text.

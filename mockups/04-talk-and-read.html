WORLDWALKER MOCKUP 4 of 10

Talk and read (Episode 4.1)

+1 🌱 PEOPLE & LAND

Tap a yellow word to see what it means. Try the language buttons, "Make it easier", then answer Aisha.

WHAT THE STUDENT SEES

A real place drawn as a game screen: dunes of the Sahara at the top, dry Sahel grass in the middle, greener savanna at the bottom. Walking into Aisha opens her talk box with her face, her words, and language buttons.

WHAT THEY LEARN HERE

  • The Sahel sits between the Sahara and the savanna
  • Dry years + too many animals eating grass = desertification
  • Key words: Sahel, savanna, desertification

HOW IT PLAYS

Every talk is short and at about a grade 5 reading level. Yellow words open a one-line meaning. EN / ES / دری swaps the whole talk box, including the meanings and the answers; Dari reads right to left. "Make it easier" gives a shorter version with the same idea.

Then the student answers in their own voice. Every choice gets a reason, right or wrong. A right answer fills the 🌱 People & land meter by one; a wrong one just lets them try again (no hearts lost while reading).

STANDARDS

WG.7 Sub-Saharan AfricaWG.2 regions

BUILT WITH

Aisha, the cattle, the well and the acacia trees are drawn in code. All three languages live in the episode's text file, next to each other, so a teacher can fix a word without touching the game.

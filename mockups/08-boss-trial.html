WHAT THE STUDENT SEES

The Storm Smudge rises over South Asia at the end of Episode 6.1, riding the summer rains. Its cloud shield has 3 pieces. Each right answer cracks one piece. When the shield breaks, the atlas page for South Asia drops.

WHAT THEY LEARN HERE

  • Summer monsoon winds blow from the ocean to the land and bring rain
  • Reading wind arrows on a map
  • Why farmers in South Asia depend on the rainy season

HOW IT PLAYS

The boss is a mastery check in disguise. Its questions come from the unit's mastery-check question bank, so practice in the game matches the real check. Every wrong answer shows the reason and goes back into the pile. Missed questions come back before the episode ends, so students see them twice.

Run out of hearts? Pip pulls you back to the last village with full hearts. Nobody gets stuck.

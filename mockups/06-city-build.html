Click a worker, then click a field, forest or rock to gather. Click Survey site and walk onto a glowing circle to fill in its column of the report. Build the town hall on the site you can defend best with the report.

WHAT THE STUDENT SEES

A wide map seen from above, like the first Warcraft games. The map scrolls when you move the mouse to the edge. A brown command panel at the bottom shows the chosen worker, resources, three big buttons and a minimap.

WHAT THEY LEARN HERE

  • Site = the land itself (flat, high, wet, rocky)
  • Situation = what is around it and how it connects (sea, river, roads, neighbors)
  • Why so many big East Asian cities sit on river plains near the sea

HOW IT PLAYS

Workers gather food, water and wood. Surveying all three circles fills the report. There is no single "right" circle: the student picks one and must explain the choice with one site reason and one situation reason. Then the town grows over a few turns, and a flood or a trade boat shows what the choice meant. This fills 📍 Where and 🚚 On the move.

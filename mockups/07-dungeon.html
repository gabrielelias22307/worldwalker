In the game the student steps on the glowing floor switches to move the gates. In this mockup you can click a switch on the map or use the buttons.

WHAT THE STUDENT SEES

One room of a temple dungeon, like the first Zelda. A river pours in from the top. Stone channels carry the water to three farm plots. The top strip shows the item just earned (the Water Key) and a map of the temple's rooms.

WHAT THEY LEARN HERE

  • Shared rivers cross borders: the Tigris and Euphrates start in Türkiye and flow into Syria and Iraq
  • Whoever controls water upstream (dams) affects everyone downstream
  • Sharing water is a reason countries argue and a reason they work together

HOW IT PLAYS

Two floor switches move the gates. Hold the dam shut and only the upstream farm grows. Open it halfway and there is only enough water for one more farm. The puzzle is solved only when every farm gets some water. Solving it fills 🌱 People & land and 🧩 Regions and opens the door to the next room. Later rooms add oil wells and desert springs.

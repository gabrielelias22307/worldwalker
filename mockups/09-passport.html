WHAT THE STUDENT SEES

A passport that grows across the whole course: their hero, the five geography powers, one tool from each realm, and a stamp for every episode they finish.

WHAT EACH POWER TRACKS

  • 📍 Where (Location) — map skills, latitude and longitude, finding places
  • 🏞️ What it is like (Place) — land, climate, plants and people of a place
  • 🌱 People & land (Human–Environment Interaction) — how people change land and land changes people
  • 🚚 On the move (Movement) — trade, migration, ideas spreading
  • 🧩 Regions (Region) — sorting places that share traits

Each right answer, quest and challenge adds to one power. A low power tells the student (and you) what to practice.

HOW IT CARRIES OVER

The class Google Sheet is the main save. If it is down or blocked, the Passport Code holds the same facts in 12 letters and numbers. The browser also keeps its own copy. Three saves, so nobody loses their hero.

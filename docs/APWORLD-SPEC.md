# Silkwalker · AP World History: Modern, Units 1–4 (c. 1200–1750)

A sixth game in the Worldwalker family (working name "Silkwalker"; easy to rename). Same tested engine (movement,
saving, talk, challenges, the Grump's trial, Passport Code, Copy my work), with three big changes:
- **One file.** The whole game, all 26 episodes and a world map, is one HTML file. No size limit.
- **New art.** A detailed "HD pixel" look (below).
- **AP level, English only.** Real AP words and AP-style stimulus questions.

## 1. Learning card
- Course: AP World History: Modern, Units 1–4, one episode per CED topic (26 episodes).
- Big idea: between 1200 and 1750, states grew and changed, and trade, belief and conquest tied the world's
  regions closer together, for better and for worse.
- Must-know words: chosen per topic from the CED (e.g. 1.1 Neo-Confucianism, filial piety, Champa rice, tributary
  system, bureaucracy, civil service exam).
- Wrong ideas to break: "Europe led the world before 1450"; "trade only moved goods" (it moved ideas, religions
  and disease too); "empires ruled by force alone" (they also used belief, bureaucracy and local elites).
- Afterward a student can: answer AP stimulus multiple choice on the topic, and explain one cause, one effect or
  one comparison in a sentence that uses evidence (the reflection).

## 2. Feel card
- Feelings: curious, clever, connected.
- Never lost: progress (saved after every goal), hearts while reading. Run out of hearts → full hearts where you stand.
- Always there: hints from the guide, retries, coins.
- Softness: warm painted palette, day–evening light, gentle weather, chip music with an off switch, slow pace.
  The Grump's helpers sleep until you talk to someone (the calm start from the other games).

## 3. Mechanic card (per AP reasoning skill)
| AP skill | Game action | Engine challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Comparison | Carry each card to the state or region it fits | `carry` |
| Causation | Pull the levers of causes; the world shows the effect | `levers` |
| Continuity and change | Fix a broken timeline / route in order | `route`, `timeline` |
| Claim with evidence | Gather sources, then pick a claim and 1 reason from each list | `site` / `claim` |
| Sourcing (HIPP) | Survey posts: who wrote it, why, for whom | `survey` |
Delete test: every challenge is the historical thinking itself, not a quiz with a game around it.

## 4. Beat sheet (about 30–40 minutes, one class block)
1. Arrive: an opening scene on the world map (place and year), what the Grump blurred.
2. Explore and talk: 3–4 people of that time and place (ordinary people; famous people only mentioned), one AP
   idea and one question each.
3. Main challenge: the mechanic above.
4. Trial: 8 Grump questions, AP style: most open with a short stimulus (a source excerpt with its author and date,
   a small table or map) and ask about cause, comparison, continuity or the source's point of view. Wrong answers
   explain and come back.
5. Reward: learned lines, a reflection with a sentence starter, Passport Code, Copy my work.

## 5. World card
- Hub: a painted world map (Afro-Eurasia and the Americas) with 26 glowing stops, unit by unit. Units open in order;
  Gabriel can open all with the teacher code.
- Each episode: the real place on a 32 × 22 map (2 × 2 screens), e.g. 1.1 Hangzhou, 1.2 Baghdad's House of Wisdom
  and Cairo, 2.1 Samarkand, 2.3 Calicut, 2.4 Timbuktu, 3.1 Istanbul, 4.3 a Caribbean port.
- Locks and keys, guide arrow, goal list: as in the other games.

## 6. Script
- AP reading level, English only. Tap-to-define gold words stay (AP terms).
- At most about 25 words per talk box; stimulus excerpts up to about 60 words.

## 7. Evidence card
- Logged: talk answers, challenge choices, trial questions missed, hints, minutes.
- Turned in: Passport Code + Copy my work text (stars, missed questions, reflection).
- Reflection: an AP-style prompt, e.g. "Explain one way the Song dynasty used Confucian ideas to rule."
  Starter: "The Song dynasty … This shows …"

## 8. Build plan
- **Phase 1 – the look and the file.** New art layer: tiles drawn at 2× detail (grass blades, stone, roof tiles,
  shaded water with waves and reflections), soft shadows, warm light and evening glow, lanterns that light the dark,
  weather (rain, blossoms, dust), birds, fish and smoke, bigger animated people. One-file bundle with the world map
  hub. Episode 1.1 (Song China) as the model. Screenshots before going on.
- **Phase 2 – Unit 1** (1.2–1.7). **Phase 3 – Unit 2** (2.1–2.7). **Phase 4 – Units 3 and 4** (3.1–4.8).
- **Phase 5 – play test** every episode, check AP facts and questions, fix.
- The new art stays a switch in the engine, so the other five games can get it later if Gabriel wants.
